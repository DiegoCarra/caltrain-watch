HOW THE ANSWER IS MADE

No mystery confidence score.

1Official timing

511 GTFS-realtime gives the baseline delay and cancellations.

2Independent reports

We cluster recent reports by train, location, and cause instead of treating every tip equally.

3Evidence agreement

Confidence rises when separate riders agree or official alerts corroborate the same event.

4Contradictions stay visible

If the board says 25 minutes and the feed says 10, we show both instead of averaging the disagreement away.
